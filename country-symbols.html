The State Name

Though pressed for time after liberation, Kim Il Sung paid deep attention to the issue of naming the state. In consideration of the reality that Korea was not yet reunified and of the immediate task and main purpose of the Korean revolution, he said it was most reasonable to call the country the Democratic People's Republic of Korea.

The name embodies the aspiration and will of the Korean people to build a democratic and independent reunified state, and their pride and dignity as the masters of the country.

National Emblem

The national emblem of the DPRK was enacted on September 9, Juche 37 (1948) along with the founding of the DPRK. It is stipulated by the Socialist Constitution of the DPRK.

Mt Paektu, the sacred mountain of the revolution, symbolizes the revolutionary cause of Juche pioneered by President Kim Il Sung, founding father of socialist Korea, and the glorious revolutionary traditions. The five-pointed red star and its beaming light represent the bright vista for the country and the ideals of the Korean people.

National Flag

The national flag of the DPRK was enacted on September 9, Juche 37 (1948) along with the founding of the DPRK. It is stipulated by the Socialist Constitution of the DPRK.

The red colour of the flag symbolizes the anti-Japanese revolutionary spirit and the patriotic spirit of the Korean people, and the red star stands for the revolutionary traditions and the bright future of the country. The white stripes represent purity and the blue stripes signify sovereignty, peace and friendship.

National Anthem

The national anthem of the DPRK is "The Patriotic Song." It was composed by Kim Won Gyun, with words by Ri Chan. The anthem expresses the pride and will of the Korean people to build a powerful, independent and sovereign state.

National Flower — Magnolia

The magnolia (Magnolia sieboldii) is the national flower of the DPRK. It is native to the Korean peninsula and blooms in spring with pure white flowers. The magnolia symbolises the noble soul and purity of the Korean people.

National Bird — Goshawk

The goshawk is the national bird of the DPRK. It is known for its sharp vision, courage and agility, symbolising the bold and indomitable spirit of the Korean people.

National Tree — Pine

The pine tree is the national tree of the DPRK. Evergreen through all seasons, it symbolises the steadfast and unyielding character of the Korean nation that remains firm and vigorous even in the most difficult conditions.

National Dog — Pungsan

The Pungsan is the national dog of the DPRK, a breed originating from the Pungsan region in the northern part of the Korean peninsula. It is renowned for its loyalty, courage and endurance.
