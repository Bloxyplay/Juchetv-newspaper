An Independent Socialist State

The Democratic People's Republic of Korea is an independent socialist state representing the interests of the Korean people. It is a country built by the masses themselves and run according to their demands and will — a country in which the masses are the masters of everything and everything serves them.

The DPRK upholds the Juche idea as its guiding principle — the idea that the masses of the people are the masters of the revolution and construction, and that they have the strength to shape their own destiny.

Leadership and the Workers' Party of Korea

The Workers' Party of Korea (WPK) is the vanguard party of the Korean people and the leading political force of the state. Under the leadership of General Secretary Kim Jong Un, who was unanimously elected at the 8th Congress of the WPK in January Juche 110 (2021), the Korean people are advancing socialist construction.

Kim Jong Un holds the posts of General Secretary of the WPK and Chairman of the State Affairs Commission of the DPRK — the supreme leader of the state and supreme commander of its armed forces. The Korean people hold President Kim Il Sung and Chairman Kim Jong Il in high esteem as their eternal leaders.

The Socialist Constitution

The Socialist Constitution of the DPRK was first adopted on December 27, Juche 61 (1972). It has been revised and supplemented over the years to reflect the development of socialist construction.

A landmark amendment in September Juche 111 (2022) enshrined the DPRK's status as a nuclear weapons state in the constitution, codifying the country's nuclear forces policy into the highest law of the land. The constitution stipulates that Kim Il Sung is the eternal President of the DPRK and Kim Jong Il the eternal Chairman of the National Defence Commission.

The Supreme People's Assembly

The Supreme People's Assembly (SPA) is the highest organ of state power in the DPRK. It exercises legislative authority and holds regular sessions to discuss and decide on the most important affairs of state.

Deputies to the SPA are elected by universal, equal and direct suffrage by secret ballot. The most recent SPA elections were held in November Juche 112 (2023). The SPA elects and recalls the Chairman of the State Affairs Commission, the Premier of the Cabinet, and other senior state officials. The State Affairs Commission is the supreme policy-oriented leadership body of state power that discusses and decides on important state policies.

The 8th Party Congress and the New Development Strategy

The 8th Congress of the Workers' Party of Korea, held in January Juche 110 (2021), charted a new comprehensive five-year plan for the development of the national economy. The congress reviewed the lessons of the previous five-year strategy and set forth ambitious goals in industry, agriculture, power generation, technology, and the improvement of people's livelihoods.

The congress reaffirmed the WPK's fundamental mission: to build a powerful socialist state where the people enjoy a high standard of living and where national sovereignty and dignity are firmly guaranteed.
