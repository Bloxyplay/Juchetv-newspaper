Preamble

The Democratic People's Republic of Korea is a people-centred socialist state that represents the interests of the Korean people and struggles for the cause of socialism. The DPRK takes Kimilsungism-Kimjongilism as its sole guiding principle for state construction and activity, and sets the Kimilsungist-Kimjongilist transformation of all society as the overall direction and goal of state construction. The DPRK firmly upholds, inherits and continuously develops the glorious traditions established in the revolutionary struggle for national liberation and the people's freedom and happiness. The DPRK sets as its fundamental principle of state construction: the establishment of the leader's monolithic leadership system, adherence to the independent revolutionary line, and the thorough realisation of the principle of putting the popular masses first. The DPRK organises and carries forward all state affairs in accordance with the leader's ideas and intentions, and firmly upholds and perpetually glorifies the leader's state-building ideas and achievements. The DPRK maintains an independent stand in state construction and activity, and develops all spheres of social life in its own way and through its own efforts. The DPRK places the demands and interests of the popular masses first and regards the enhancement of the people's wellbeing as its supreme principle. The DPRK holds fast to the general line of socialist construction: to strengthen the people's government while raising its functions and role, and simultaneously to carry out thoroughly the three revolutions in ideology, technology and culture. The DPRK continuously strengthens the unified leadership and management of the people's government over all of society in line with the demands of the changing era and revolutionary practice. The DPRK raises the guiding role of the state in carrying out the three revolutions, accelerating the revolutionisation of all social members, the elevation of technical and economic capacity, and the civilisation of all society. The DPRK thoroughly implements the class line and mass line, strengthens single-hearted unity by all means, resolves all problems by relying on the patriotic enthusiasm and creativity of the popular masses, and strengthens the people's democratic dictatorship to reliably defend the socialist system and the rights and interests of the people. The DPRK strengthens the People's Army, realises the arming of all the people and the fortification of the entire country at a higher level, develops the defence industry, and continuously enhances national defence capability. The DPRK improves and completes the legal system and strengthens law-abiding life, so that the law protects the people and the people abide by the law. The DPRK holds fast to independence, peace and friendship as the basic principles of its foreign policy and the protection of national interest as its invariable principle, expands and develops external relations, and struggles for world peace and security and the common prosperity of all humanity. The DPRK will firmly hold fast to the Constitution — the true legal code of the people and a powerful political weapon for revolution and construction — and will fulfil the Juche revolutionary cause to the very end.

Chapter 1 — Politics

Article 1

The name of our state is the Democratic People's Republic of Korea.

Article 2

The territory of the DPRK encompasses the land bordering the People's Republic of China and the Russian Federation to the north and the Republic of Korea to the south, together with the territorial sea and airspace established on that basis. The DPRK shall never permit any infringement of its territory.

Article 3

A citizen of the DPRK is a person holding DPRK nationality. Citizens are protected by the DPRK regardless of their place of residence or sojourn.

Article 4

Sovereignty in the DPRK belongs to the working people, including workers, farmers, soldiers and intellectuals. The working people exercise sovereignty through their representative organs — the Supreme People's Assembly and the local People's Assemblies at all levels.

Article 5

All state organs in the DPRK are organised and function on the principle of democratic centralism.

Article 6

State power organs at all levels, from county People's Assembly up to the Supreme People's Assembly, are elected by universal, equal and direct election through secret ballot.

Article 7

Deputies to state power organs at all levels maintain close ties with their electors and are accountable to them. Electors may recall deputies who have lost their confidence at any time.

Article 8

The social system of the DPRK is one centred on people, in which the working masses are the masters of everything and everything in society serves them. The state upholds the interests of the working people and respects and protects human rights.

Article 9

The DPRK conducts all its activities under the leadership of the Korean Workers' Party.

Article 10

The DPRK is based on the political and ideological unity of the entire people solidly united around the Korean Workers' Party. The state strengthens the ideological revolution, making all members of society revolutionary and working-class in character, transforming all society into a collective bound together in comradeship.

Article 11

The state vigorously conducts mass revolutionary movements and patriotic movements, including the movement to win the three-revolution red flag, and maximally accelerates socialist construction.

Article 12

The DPRK protects the democratic national rights and the lawful rights and interests recognised by international law of Korean compatriots living abroad.

Article 13

The DPRK guarantees the lawful rights and interests of foreigners within its territory.

Article 14

The DPRK establishes state, political, economic and cultural relations with all friendly countries on the principles of complete equality, independence, mutual respect, non-interference in internal affairs, and mutual benefit. The state strengthens solidarity with peoples aspiring to independence and justice, opposes all forms of aggression and interference in other countries' internal affairs, and actively supports the struggle of all peoples to safeguard national sovereignty and achieve national and class liberation.

Article 15

The law of the DPRK is an expression of the will and interests of the working people and the basic instrument of state administration. Respect and strict observance and enforcement of the law is an obligation for all organs, enterprises, organisations and citizens.

Chapter 2 — The Economy

Article 16

The DPRK is based on socialist relations of production and a self-reliant national economy.

Article 17

In the DPRK, the means of production are owned by the state and social cooperative organisations.

Article 18

State ownership is the ownership of the entire people. The scope of state ownership is unlimited. All natural resources, railways, air transport, communications and major factories and enterprises, ports and banks — the fundamental lifeblood of the national economy — are owned exclusively by the state. The state prioritises the protection and growth of state ownership, which plays a leading role in national economic development.

Article 19

Social cooperative ownership is the collective ownership of the working people belonging to the relevant cooperative organisation. Land, farm machinery, vessels and small-to-medium factories and enterprises may be owned by social cooperative organisations. The state protects social cooperative ownership.

Article 20

The state raises the ideological and technical-cultural level of farmers; organically combines the two forms of ownership; improves the guidance and management of cooperative farming; consolidates and develops the socialist cooperative farming system; and gradually converts cooperative ownership to all-people ownership in accordance with the voluntary wishes of all members of cooperative organisations.

Article 21

Personal ownership is ownership for the personal and consumer purposes of citizens. Personal ownership is derived from socialist distribution according to labour and additional benefits from the state and society. Income lawfully obtained by individuals also constitutes personal property. The state protects personal ownership and legally guarantees inheritance rights.

Article 22

The DPRK regards the continuous improvement of the material and cultural life of the people as its supreme principle. The growing material wealth of our society is directed entirely to the enhancement of working people's welfare. The state struggles to provide the people with abundant and civilised living conditions.

Article 23

The self-reliant national economy of the DPRK is a firm foundation for the happy lives of the people and the prosperity of the homeland. The state holds fast to the line of socialist self-reliant national economic construction, drives forward the modernisation, informatisation and scientification of the national economy, and struggles to develop it into a highly advanced Juche economy befitting a fully victorious socialist society.

Article 24

Technological revolution is the key link for developing the socialist economy; scientific and technological capability is the state's most important strategic resource. The state enhances the leading role of science and technology in all economic activities, integrates science and technology with production, and vigorously conducts the mass technical innovation movement to accelerate economic construction.

Article 25

The state drives forward the agricultural technological revolution to eliminate the differences between town and countryside and between the working class and the peasantry; industrialises and modernises agriculture; and strengthens guidance and assistance to rural areas. The state increases state guarantees and support for rural areas, strengthens the material and technical foundation of agriculture, and provides excellent living conditions for rural residents.

Article 26

The state drives forward the autonomous and diversified development of cities and counties, turning every city and county into a strategic base of the civilised and powerful socialist state with its own characteristic features. The state actively develops and effectively utilises local natural and economic resources, accelerates the modernisation and informatisation of local industrial factories, and ensures simultaneous and balanced development based primarily on local characteristics.

Article 27

Socialism is built through the patriotic enthusiasm and creative labour of the working masses. Labour in the DPRK is the autonomous and creative labour of working people. The state makes the labour of workers a more joyful and rewarding endeavour in which they work with conscious enthusiasm and creative zeal for society, the collective and themselves.

Article 28

The working day for labourers is eight hours. The state may reduce daily working hours in accordance with the degree of difficulty and other special conditions of labour. The state organises labour well, strengthens labour discipline, and ensures the full utilisation of working hours.

Article 29

The minimum working age for citizens of the DPRK is 17. The state prohibits the labour of minors who have not reached working age.

Article 30

In leading and managing the socialist economy, the state firmly adheres to the principle of correctly combining: political guidance with economic and technical guidance; unified state leadership with the creativity of individual units; unified command with democracy; and political-moral incentives with material incentives, while ensuring practical benefit.

Article 31

The state manages the economy scientifically and rationally by relying on the collective wisdom and strength of the producing masses, and decisively raises the role of the Cabinet. In economic management, the state implements the socialist enterprise responsibility management system and correctly utilises economic levers such as cost, price and profitability.

Article 32

The national economy of the DPRK is a planned economy. The state draws up and implements national economic development plans to achieve the correct balance between accumulation and consumption, accelerate economic construction, continuously raise the standard of living of the people, and strengthen national defence capability. The state realises the unification and detail-orientation of planning to ensure a high rate of growth in production and balanced development of the national economy.

Article 33

The DPRK draws up and executes the state budget in accordance with the national economic development plan. The state intensifies the campaign for increased production and economy in all sectors, rigorously implements financial control, systematically increases state accumulation, and expands and develops socialist ownership.

Article 34

Foreign trade in the DPRK is conducted by state organs, enterprises and social cooperative organisations. The state upholds credibility in foreign trade, improves the trade structure, and expands and develops external economic relations on the principles of equality and mutual benefit.

Article 35

The state encourages joint ventures and cooperation between our country's organs, enterprises and organisations and foreign legal persons or individuals, and encourages the establishment and operation of various enterprises in special economic zones.

Article 36

The state implements a tariff policy to protect the self-reliant national economy.

Chapter 3 — Culture

Article 37

The flourishing socialist culture of the DPRK serves to enhance the creative abilities of working people and meet their healthy cultural and emotional needs.

Article 38

The DPRK thoroughly carries out the cultural revolution, making all people socialist builders equipped with deep knowledge of nature and society and a high level of cultural and technical skills, and accelerates the scientific and technical talent-development of the entire people.

Article 39

The DPRK builds a genuinely popular and revolutionary culture that serves socialist workers. In building socialist national culture, the state opposes imperialist cultural infiltration and, on the principles of self-reliance, historicism and scientism, protects the national cultural heritage and inherits and develops it in accordance with socialist reality.

Article 40

In scientific research, the state establishes Juche, actively absorbs advanced science and technology, increases state investment in the scientific research sector, pioneers new fields of science and technology, and raises the country's science and technology to world-class level.

Article 41

The state draws up correct plans for the development of science and technology, establishes strict discipline for their thorough implementation, and reinforces creative cooperation between scientists, technicians and producers.

Article 42

The state implements the principles of socialist pedagogy, rearing the younger generation into true patriots who struggle for society, the collective, the homeland and the people, and into main forces of socialist construction with intellectual, moral and physical qualities.

Article 43

The state puts people's education and the training of national cadres ahead of all other work, and closely integrates general education with technical education and education with productive labour. The state improves the structure, content and methods of education and develops education into the most excellent and ideal education to which the future of our people can be entrusted with confidence.

Article 44

The state develops universal 12-year compulsory education — including one year of preschool compulsory education — at a high level in accordance with trends in modern science and technology and the requirements of socialist construction.

Article 45

The state develops full-time and work-study educational systems; continuously improves educational conditions and the environment; and cultivates capable scientific and technical talent.

Article 46

The state provides free education for all students, grants scholarships to university students, and expands and strengthens socialist measures for the younger generation.

Article 47

The state strengthens social education and ensures all conditions for all working people to study.

Article 48

The state raises preschool children in nurseries and kindergartens at state and social expense.

Article 49

The state consolidates and develops the socialist healthcare system, improves the quality of medical services, and strengthens the material and technical foundation of the healthcare sector so as to protect lives and promote the health of working people.

Article 50

The state develops Juche-oriented revolutionary literature and arts with national form and socialist content. The state encourages creative workers and artists to produce works of high ideological and artistic value, and encourages broad participation of the masses in cultural and artistic activities.

Article 51

The state equips the country with sufficient modern cultural facilities in keeping with people's desire for continuous spiritual and physical development, so that all working people may enjoy socialist cultural life to the full.

Article 52

The state makes physical culture a mass activity and part of everyday life, preparing the entire people for labour and national defence, and develops sports technology in accordance with national conditions and trends in modern sports technology.

Article 53

The state creates and develops a revolutionary and noble socialist lifestyle culture and intensifies the struggle against phenomena inconsistent with the socialist way of life.

Article 54

The state ensures that all members of society maintain independence and national character in their linguistic life, and protects and actively promotes the Pyongyang cultural language.

Article 55

The state takes environmental protection measures prior to production, preserves and improves the natural environment, prevents environmental pollution, and provides the people with a hygienic living environment and working conditions.

Chapter 4 — National Defence

Article 56

The DPRK relies on an all-people, all-state defence system. As a responsible nuclear-armed state, the DPRK advances the development of nuclear weapons to a higher level in order to guarantee the state's right to existence and development, deter war, and safeguard peace and stability in the region and the world.

Article 57

The mission of the armed forces of the DPRK is to defend national sovereignty, territorial integrity and the rights and interests of the people; safeguard the socialist system and the gains of the revolution from all threats; and guarantee the peace and prosperity of the homeland through powerful military force.

Article 58

The state, on the basis of arming the people and People's Army soldiers politically and ideologically, implements the self-defence military line, the basic content of which is to make all soldiers cadres, modernise the entire army, arm all the people, and fortify the entire country.

Article 59

The state establishes a revolutionary command system and military ethos within the army; strengthens military discipline and mass discipline; and promotes the noble traditional virtues of unity between officers and men, coordination of military and political work, and unity between the military and the people.

Article 60

The state develops defence science and technology and continuously raises the level of self-reliance, modernisation and scientification of the defence industry.

Article 61

The state establishes a military-first culture throughout society and thoroughly prepares for an all-people resistance war.

Chapter 5 — Fundamental Rights and Duties of Citizens

Article 62

The rights and duties of citizens of the DPRK are based on the collectivist principle of 'One for all, all for one.'

Article 63

The state genuinely guarantees all citizens true democratic rights and freedoms and a happy material and cultural life. The rights and freedoms of citizens of the DPRK are further expanded with the consolidation and development of the socialist system.

Article 64

Citizens enjoy equal rights in all spheres of state and social life.

Article 65

All citizens aged 18 and above have the right to vote and to be elected, regardless of sex, ethnicity, occupation, length of residence, property status, level of education, party affiliation, political views or religious belief. Citizens serving in the armed forces also have these rights. Persons deprived of the right to vote by court verdict, and persons with mental illness, do not enjoy these rights.

Article 66

Citizens have freedom of speech, the press, assembly, demonstration and association. The state guarantees conditions for the free activities of democratic political parties and social organisations.

Article 67

Citizens have freedom of religious belief. This right is guaranteed by permitting the construction of religious buildings and the holding of religious ceremonies. Religion must not be used to introduce foreign forces or to harm the state and social order.

Article 68

Citizens may submit grievances and petitions. The state shall examine and process them fairly and in accordance with the provisions of the law.

Article 69

Citizens have the right to work. All citizens with the ability to work have the right to choose their occupation according to their wishes and abilities, and are guaranteed stable employment and working conditions. Citizens work according to their ability and receive remuneration according to the quantity and quality of their labour.

Article 70

Citizens have the right to rest. This right is guaranteed by the working hours system, the public holiday system, the paid leave system, the rest and recuperation system at state expense, and continuously expanding cultural facilities.

Article 71

Citizens have the right to medical care. Persons who have lost the ability to work due to old age, illness or disability, and elderly persons and children without anyone to care for them, have the right to receive material assistance. This right is guaranteed by expanding modern medical facilities and the state social insurance and social security system.

Article 72

Citizens have the right to education. This right is guaranteed by the advanced educational system and the state's people-oriented education policy.

Article 73

Citizens have freedom of scientific and literary-artistic activities. The state takes care of citizens who have contributed to the development of science and literature and the arts. The state protects the intellectual property rights of citizens by law.

Article 74

Citizens have freedom of residence and travel.

Article 75

Heroes, war veterans, wartime meritorious persons, honoured soldiers, families of revolutionary and patriotic martyrs, surviving families of martyrs from overseas military operations, socialist patriotic meritorious persons, retired generals, hereditary military officers, and families of People's Army soldiers receive special protection from the state and society.

Article 76

Women enjoy the same social status and rights as men. The state specially protects mothers and children through guaranteed prenatal and postnatal leave, preferential treatment for mothers with multiple children, expansion of maternity hospitals, nurseries and kindergartens, and other measures. The state creates all conditions for women to participate in social life.

Article 77

Marriage and the family are under the protection of the state. The state pays profound attention to the consolidation of the family — the basic unit of social life.

Article 78

Citizens are guaranteed inviolability of person and home, and the secrecy of correspondence. Citizens may not be detained or arrested, nor may their homes be searched, without legal process.

Article 79

The DPRK grants protection to foreigners who have fled to our country while struggling for peace and democracy, national independence and socialism, or for freedom of scientific and cultural activity.

Article 80

Citizens must firmly defend the political and ideological unity and solidarity of the entire people. Citizens must value organisations and the collective, and display the spirit of selfless devotion to society and the people.

Article 81

Citizens must abide by the laws of the state and socialist norms of life, and must uphold the honour and dignity of DPRK citizens.

Article 82

Labour is the sacred duty and honour of citizens. Citizens must conscientiously and faithfully participate in labour, and strictly observe labour discipline and working hours.

Article 83

Citizens must cherish and love state property and social cooperative property, struggle against all acts of corruption and waste, and manage the country's economic life responsibly as its masters. State property and social cooperative property are inviolable.

Article 84

Citizens must always sharpen their revolutionary vigilance and struggle selflessly for the security of the state.

Article 85

Defending the homeland is the highest duty and greatest honour of citizens. Citizens must defend the homeland and must serve in the armed forces as prescribed by law.

Chapter 6 — State Institutions: Chairman & Supreme People's Assembly

Section 1 — Chairman of the State Affairs Commission

Article 86

The Chairman of the State Affairs Commission of the DPRK is the head of state representing the DPRK.

Article 87

The Chairman of the State Affairs Commission is elected at the Supreme People's Assembly in accordance with the unanimous will of all the Korean people. The Chairman is not elected as a deputy to the Supreme People's Assembly.

Article 88

The term of office of the Chairman of the State Affairs Commission is the same as that of the Supreme People's Assembly.

Article 89

The Chairman of the State Affairs Commission is the Supreme Commander of the armed forces of the DPRK and commands and directs all state armed forces. The command authority over nuclear forces belongs to the Chairman of the State Affairs Commission. The Chairman may also delegate the authority to use nuclear forces to the state nuclear force command organ.

Article 90

The Chairman of the State Affairs Commission has the following powers: 1. To lead all state affairs. 2. During recesses of the Supreme People's Assembly, to suspend, appoint or dismiss important state officials including the Chairman of the SPA and the Premier of the Cabinet. 3. To cause deputies who have lost the confidence of the people to resign. 4. To exercise the right of veto over laws, decrees, decisions and instructions not in keeping with state development and the demands of the people. 5. To confer state commendations on distinguished meritorious individuals. 6. To receive credentials from foreign diplomatic representatives. 7. To appoint or recall diplomatic representatives resident in other countries. 8. To ratify or abrogate important treaties with other countries. 9. To exercise the power of special pardon. 10. To declare a state of emergency, a state of war, and to issue mobilisation orders. 11. To organise and lead the national defence commission in time of war.

Article 91

The Chairman of the State Affairs Commission issues orders and decrees.

Section 2 — Supreme People's Assembly

Article 92

The Supreme People's Assembly is the highest organ of state power of the DPRK.

Article 93

The Supreme People's Assembly exercises legislative power. During recesses, its Presidium may also exercise legislative power.

Article 94

The Supreme People's Assembly is composed of deputies elected by universal, equal and direct election through secret ballot.

Article 95

The term of the Supreme People's Assembly is five years. New elections shall be held before the expiry of the current term, by decision of the Presidium. If elections cannot be held on time due to unavoidable circumstances, the term is extended until elections are held.

Article 96

The Supreme People's Assembly has the following powers: 1. To amend and supplement the Constitution. 2. To enact or amend and supplement sectoral laws. 3. To approve important sectoral laws adopted by the Presidium during recesses. 4. To establish the basic principles of state domestic and foreign policy. 5. To elect the Chairman of the State Affairs Commission. 6. To elect or recall members of the State Affairs Commission on the Chairman's recommendation. 7. To elect or recall the Chairman and Vice-Chairmen of the SPA. 8. To elect or recall the Secretary-General and members of the SPA Presidium. 9. To elect or recall the Premier of the Cabinet. 10. To appoint or dismiss Cabinet members on the Premier's recommendation. 11. To appoint or dismiss the Procurator-General of the Supreme Procuratorate. 12. To elect or recall the President of the Supreme Court. 13. To elect or recall members of specialised committees. 14–17. To approve national economic and budget plans and reports; to establish or abolish Cabinet committees and provinces; to hear work reports; and to decide on treaty ratification or abrogation.

Article 97

The SPA holds regular and extraordinary sessions. Regular sessions are convened twice a year by the Presidium. Extraordinary sessions are convened when the Presidium deems it necessary or when requested by one-third or more of all deputies.

Article 98

The SPA is constituted when two-thirds or more of all deputies are present.

Article 99

The SPA is presided over by the Chairman; in the absence of the Chairman, by the Vice-Chairman.

Article 100

Items for deliberation at the SPA may be submitted by the Chairman of the State Affairs Commission, the State Affairs Commission, the Presidium, the Cabinet and the SPA specialised committees. Deputies may also submit items.

Article 101

The SPA issues laws and decisions, adopted when approved by more than half of the deputies present. Amendments to the Constitution require the approval of two-thirds or more of all deputies.

Article 102

The SPA establishes necessary specialised committees including a Legislative Committee, a Budget Committee and a Foreign Affairs Committee. During recesses, specialised committees work under the guidance of the Presidium.

Article 103

Deputies to the SPA enjoy inviolability. Except in cases of flagrante delicto, deputies may not be arrested or subjected to criminal punishment without the approval of the SPA or, during its recess, its Presidium.

Chapter 6 — State Institutions: State Affairs Commission, Presidium & Cabinet

Section 3 — State Affairs Commission

Article 104

The State Affairs Commission is the supreme policy-leading organ of state power.

Article 105

The State Affairs Commission is composed of the Chairman, the First Vice-Chairman, Vice-Chairmen and members.

Article 106

The term of the State Affairs Commission is the same as that of the Supreme People's Assembly.

Article 107

The State Affairs Commission has the following duties and powers: 1. To deliberate upon and decide on major state policies. 2. To supervise implementation of the Chairman's orders and decrees and the Commission's decisions and instructions, and to take measures accordingly. 3. To annul decisions and instructions of state organs that conflict with these. 4. During recesses of the SPA, to appoint or dismiss Cabinet members on the recommendation of the Premier.

Article 108

The State Affairs Commission issues decisions and instructions.

Section 4 — Presidium of the Supreme People's Assembly

Article 109

The Presidium of the Supreme People's Assembly is the highest organ of power during recesses of the SPA.

Article 110

The Presidium is composed of a Chairman, Vice-Chairmen, a Secretary-General and members. The Chairman and Vice-Chairmen of the SPA concurrently serve as the Chairman and Vice-Chairmen of the Presidium.

Article 111

The term of the Presidium is the same as that of the SPA. The Presidium continues to perform its duties until the election of a new Presidium.

Article 112

The Presidium has the following duties and powers: 1. To convene the SPA. 2. To deliberate upon and adopt new sectoral law drafts submitted during recesses, submitting important enacted laws to the next SPA for approval. 3. To deliberate upon and approve, due to unavoidable circumstances during recesses, national economic development plans, the state budget and their adjustment plans. 4. To interpret the Constitution, current sectoral laws and regulations. 5. To supervise state organs's compliance with and enforcement of the law and take appropriate measures. 6. To annul decisions and instructions of state organs that conflict with the Constitution and relevant laws; and to halt implementation of inappropriate decisions of local People's Assemblies. 7–14. To conduct elections of SPA deputies, organise local elections, work with deputies and specialised committees, and carry out international parliamentary affairs.

Article 113

The Presidium holds plenary meetings and standing meetings.

Article 114

The Presidium issues decrees, decisions and instructions.

Article 115

The Presidium may establish specialised committees to assist its work.

Article 116

The Presidium is accountable to the Supreme People's Assembly for its work.

Section 5 — Cabinet

Article 117

The Cabinet is the administrative and executive organ of state power and the comprehensive state management organ.

Article 118

The Cabinet is composed of the Premier, the First Vice-Premier, Vice-Premiers, Committee Chairpersons, Ministers and other necessary members. The term of the Cabinet is the same as that of the SPA.

Article 119

The Cabinet has the following duties and powers: 1. To take measures for implementing state policy. 2. To enact or amend and supplement regulations pertaining to state administration on the basis of the Constitution and sectoral laws. 3. To guide the work of Cabinet committees, provinces, Cabinet-affiliated organs and local People's Committees. 4. To establish or abolish Cabinet-affiliated organs and important administrative and economic organs and enterprises, and to improve the state management structure. 5. To draw up national economic development plans and take measures for their implementation. 6. To draw up the state budget and take measures for its implementation. 7. To organise and carry out work in industry, agriculture, construction, transport, communications, commerce, trade, land management, urban management, education, science, culture, public health, physical culture, labour administration, environmental protection, tourism and other sectors. 8–12. To take measures to consolidate the monetary and banking system; to conduct inspection and supervision; to maintain social order and protect citizens' rights; to conclude treaties and conduct foreign affairs; and to annul conflicting decisions of administrative and economic organs.

Article 120

The Premier organises and leads the work of the Cabinet. The Premier represents the government of the DPRK.

Article 121

The Cabinet holds plenary meetings and standing meetings.

Article 122

The Cabinet issues decisions, administrative orders and instructions.

Article 123

The Cabinet may establish non-standing specialised committees to assist its work.

Article 124

The Cabinet is accountable to the SPA and, during recesses, to the SPA Presidium.

Article 125

A newly elected Premier takes the oath before the SPA on behalf of Cabinet members.

Article 126

Cabinet committees and provinces are the sectoral executive organs of the Cabinet and the central sectoral management organs.

Article 127

Cabinet committees and provinces, under the guidance of the Cabinet, exercise unified control and management over work in their respective sectors.

Article 128

Cabinet committees and provinces hold committee meetings and cadre meetings.

Article 129

Cabinet committees, provinces and Cabinet-affiliated organs performing sectoral management functions issue instructions.

Chapter 6 — State Institutions: Local Organs & Judiciary

Section 6 — Local People's Assemblies

Article 130

The People's Assemblies of provinces (municipalities directly under central authority), cities (districts) and counties are local organs of state power.

Article 131

Local People's Assemblies are composed of deputies elected by universal, equal and direct election through secret ballot.

Article 132

The terms of local People's Assemblies are four years.

Article 133

Local People's Assemblies have the following duties and powers: 1. To deliberate upon and approve local economic development plans and budgets and their implementation. 2. To take measures to implement state laws in their areas. 3. To elect or recall members of the relevant People's Committee. 4. To annul inappropriate decisions of the relevant People's Committee and lower organs. 5. To hear work reports from relevant organs.

Article 134

Local People's Assemblies hold regular sessions (one to two times per year) and extraordinary sessions.

Article 135

Local People's Assemblies are constituted when two-thirds or more of all deputies are present.

Article 136

Local People's Assemblies elect a chairman and a vice-chairman.

Article 137

Local People's Assemblies issue decisions.

Section 7 — Local People's Committees

Article 138

The People's Committees of provinces (municipalities directly under central authority), cities (districts) and counties are local organs of state power during recesses of the relevant People's Assembly, and the administrative and executive organs of local state power.

Article 139

Local People's Committees are composed of a chairman, vice-chairmen, a secretary-general and members. Their term is the same as that of the relevant People's Assembly.

Article 140

Local People's Committees have the following duties and powers: 1–13. To convene People's Assemblies; to organise elections; to implement higher-level orders and decrees; to organise and carry out all local administrative work; to draw up local economic plans and budgets; to take measures to maintain social order and guarantee citizens' rights; to supervise lower-level organs; and to annul inappropriate decisions and instructions of lower-level organs.

Article 141

Local People's Committees hold plenary meetings and standing meetings.

Article 142

Local People's Committees issue decisions and instructions.

Article 143

Local People's Committees may establish non-standing specialised committees to assist their work.

Article 144

Local People's Committees are accountable to the relevant People's Assembly. They work under the guidance of higher-level People's Committees, the Cabinet and the SPA Presidium.

Section 8 — Procuratorate and Courts

Article 145

The Procuratorate is the supervisory and prosecutorial organ of the DPRK.

Article 146

Procuratorial work is conducted by the Supreme Procuratorate, provincial, city, county and special procuratorates.

Article 147

The term of the Procurator-General of the Supreme Procuratorate is the same as that of the SPA.

Article 148

Procurators are appointed or dismissed by the Supreme Procuratorate.

Article 149

The Procuratorate carries out the following duties: 1. To supervise whether state organs, enterprises, organisations and citizens strictly observe state law. 2. To supervise whether decisions and instructions of state organs conform to the Constitution and higher-level laws and instructions. 3. To uncover law violators including criminals and pursue their legal responsibility, thereby protecting the sovereignty of the DPRK, the socialist system, state and social cooperative property, and the constitutional rights and lives and property of the people.

Article 150

Procuratorial work is directed uniformly by the Supreme Procuratorate; all procuratorates are subordinate to higher-level procuratorates and the Supreme Procuratorate.

Article 151

The Supreme Procuratorate issues instructions.

Article 152

The Supreme Procuratorate is accountable to the SPA and, during recesses, to its Presidium.

Article 153

Courts are the judicial organs of the DPRK.

Article 154

Judicial proceedings are conducted by the Supreme Court, provincial courts, People's Courts of cities, districts and counties, and special courts. Verdicts are pronounced in the name of the DPRK.

Article 155

The term of the President of the Supreme Court is the same as that of the SPA. The terms of judges and people's assessors of the Supreme Court, provincial courts and People's Courts are the same as those of the relevant People's Assembly.

Article 156

The president and judges of special courts are appointed or dismissed by the Supreme Court.

Article 157

Courts carry out the following duties: 1. Through judicial activities, to protect the sovereignty and socialist system of the DPRK, state and social cooperative property, and the constitutional rights and lives and property of the people. 2. To ensure that all organs, enterprises, organisations and citizens strictly observe state law and to actively struggle against class enemies and all law violators. 3. To execute judgements and rulings on property matters and to conduct notarial work.

Article 158

Proceedings are conducted by a tribunal composed of one judge and two people's assessors. In special circumstances, a tribunal of three judges may be constituted.

Article 159

Proceedings are public and the right of defence of the accused is guaranteed. Proceedings may be closed in accordance with the law.

Article 160

Proceedings are conducted in the Korean language. Foreigners may use their own language in proceedings.

Article 161

Courts conduct proceedings independently and carry out judicial activities in accordance with the law.

Article 162

The Supreme Court is the highest judicial organ of the DPRK and supervises the judicial work of all courts.

Article 163

The Supreme Court issues instructions.

Article 164

The Supreme Court is accountable to the SPA and, during recesses, to its Presidium.

Chapter 7 — National Emblem, Flag, Anthem and Capital

Article 165

The national emblem of the DPRK is an oval frame of ears of rice bound with a red ribbon bearing the inscription 'Democratic People's Republic of Korea,' with a grand hydroelectric power station in the centre, the revolutionary sacred mountain Paektu above it, and a brilliant red five-pointed star.

Article 166

The national flag of the DPRK has a broad red stripe in the centre, with a narrow white stripe above and below it, and then a blue stripe; on the side of the red stripe nearest the flagstaff there is a white circle containing a red five-pointed star. The ratio of height to length of the flag is 1:1.65.

Article 167

The national anthem of the DPRK is an all-people hymn embodying the patriotic conviction and will to pass on from generation to generation and perpetually glorify the homeland's beautiful nature, long history, brilliant culture and glorious traditions of struggle, and to build the socialist homeland under the leadership of the Korean Workers' Party into an eternal people's state and a world-class powerful nation.

Article 168

The capital of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea is Pyongyang.
